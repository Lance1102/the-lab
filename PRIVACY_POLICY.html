Privacy Policy for OrbitFlow

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how OrbitFlow ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our Chrome Extension.

TL;DR: We do not track your browsing history. Your dashboard data is stored locally on your device or in your own private Google Drive (if you enable sync). We do not have access to your personal data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Local Data Storage

OrbitFlow stores your dashboard configuration (links, groups, tabs, layout settings, custom icons) locally on your device using the chrome.storage API. This data is not sent to us; it only leaves your browser when you back it up to your own Google Drive.

Website Icons

To show an icon for each saved link, OrbitFlow loads the site's icon from Google's public favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons, served from *.gstatic.com). This request includes the address of the saved link. If you turn on Offline Icons in Settings, OrbitFlow asks for permission to these two Google hosts and keeps a copy of the icons in chrome.storage on your device so they still appear offline. Icon copies are never included in backups and can be cleared in Settings at any time.

Google Account Data (Optional Sign-in and Cloud Backup)

If you choose to sign in with Google and use the "Cloud Backup" feature:

  • We use the identity permission to facilitate authentication. Your Google account name, email address and profile picture are read to show who is signed in, and are stored only on your device.
  • Google access tokens are stored locally in chrome.storage and are used only to call Google APIs on your behalf.
  • We request access to a specialized, hidden folder in your Google Drive (Application Data folder, via https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).
  • When you press Backup, your dashboard data (groups, links and settings) is saved as a file in this private folder. It is protected by Google's own storage security; OrbitFlow does not add its own encryption.
  • We cannot see, access, or share your files in Google Drive. We can only access the specific configuration file created by OrbitFlow.

2. Permissions Explained

We request the minimum permissions necessary for the extension to function:

  • Storage: Required to save your dashboard preferences and links effectively on your computer.
  • Identity: Required only for authenticating with Google to enable the cloud backup feature.
  • Optional host access to https://www.google.com/* and https://*.gstatic.com/*: requested only when you enable Offline Icons, and used only to download website icons. You can decline, and icons will keep loading from the network.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension interacts with Google services only for sign-in, backup to your own Google Drive, and loading website icons, as described above. Those requests are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. OrbitFlow has no server of its own and does not use analytics or advertising. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties.

4. Data Retention

Since data is stored locally or in your personal Google Drive, you have full control over it. Uninstalling the extension will remove local data. You can delete the backup file from your Google Drive at any time.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Chrome Web Store support page.
